Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026 (replaces the version of April 5, 2026)

Hearken ("App", "we", "us", "our") is operated by Magomedsaip Omarov.

Contact: severrrrin@gmail.com

1. Information We Handle

  • Lecture requests: the topic you type and your lecture settings (length, language, voice, style options).
  • Generated lectures: the script text and audio created for you.
  • Voice recordings (only if you use voice cloning): three short samples you record. Copies stay in the app's private storage on your device. They are also uploaded to Fish Audio, which creates and stores a private voice model from them.
  • Content reports: the reasons you select, the identifier of the reported lecture, and an anonymous app ID.
  • Purchases: subscription status and purchase history, handled by RevenueCat with an anonymous ID. Apple processes all payments; we never see your payment details.
  • Technical data: our hosting and AI providers automatically receive your IP address, device and app version, and request times in their server logs.

We do not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools, we do not track you across apps or websites, and there is no sign-in.

2. Who Receives Your Information

  • Supabase: hosts the community catalog database, the lecture audio storage, and the server functions that relay requests to our AI providers.
  • xAI: receives your topic, settings, and the writing instructions to create the script; searches the web and X for recent topics and news; reads lectures in the xAI voices (every voice except Alex), in which case it receives the script.
  • Groq: backup script writer when xAI is unavailable; receives your topic, settings, and the writing instructions.
  • Fish Audio: reads lectures in the Alex voice and stands in when another voice is unavailable; in both cases it receives the script to turn it into speech. For voice cloning it receives your recordings and stores the voice model created from them. Fish Audio's terms allow it to use the content it processes, including scripts and voice recordings, to develop and improve its AI models.
  • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions and purchases using an anonymous ID.
  • Apple: processes payments and provides App Store services.

Each provider receives only what it needs to perform its service for us and processes it under its own terms and privacy policy. We only use providers that commit to protecting your information at least as well as this policy describes. We do not sell your information.

Before your first lecture, and again whenever this list of AI providers changes, the app asks for your permission before sending anything to them. Voice cloning has its own separate permission.

3. Public Community Catalog

Lectures made with standard voices are checked by our server and, if approved, shared to the public community catalog. Anyone using Hearken can browse, search, and listen to them, including the topic and script text. Lectures made with a cloned voice are never shared. You can hide any catalog lecture on your device and report it to us.

4. How Long We Keep Information

  • Your library, settings, and voice recordings on your device: until you delete them or delete the App.
  • Fish Audio voice models: until you delete the voice in My Voices. Deleting the voice deletes the model at Fish Audio right away, or the next time you open the App if you are offline.
  • Public catalog lectures: until we remove them, for example after a valid report or a removal request.
  • Content reports: up to 12 months.
  • AI providers may keep limited request logs under their own retention policies to operate and protect their services.
  • Server logs at our hosting provider: kept for a limited period according to its log-retention settings.
  • Purchase records: kept by Apple and RevenueCat as needed to provide your subscription and as required by law.

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • Withdraw AI consent: Adjust > AI data sharing > Reset. Nothing new is sent to AI providers until you allow it again; generating a lecture requires it.
  • Delete a cloned voice: My Voices > Delete. This also deletes the voice model at Fish Audio.
  • Hide or report a catalog lecture from the Home screen or the player.
  • Ask us to remove a published lecture, or personal information about you in one: email us with the lecture topic, or use the report option "Contains personal information about me or someone else". We review reports and requests promptly, normally within 24 hours.
  • Ask for a copy or deletion of any other information we hold about you: email us.
  • Download the text of any lecture you play.

6. Children

The App is for users 13 years and older.

7. Changes to This Policy

We will update the effective date above when this policy changes, and the App will ask for your permission again when the AI providers that receive your information change.
